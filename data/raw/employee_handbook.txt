EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK

Annual Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

Employees should submit planned leave requests at least 7 days in advance through the company's HR system.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days into the following calendar year.

Sick Leave Policy

Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year.

For sick leave lasting more than three consecutive working days, supporting medical documentation may be required.

Working Hours

Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, including a one-hour lunch break.
