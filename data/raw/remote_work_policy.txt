REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligible employees may work remotely up to three days per week.

Employees must remain available during core collaboration hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection and an appropriate working environment.

Employees handling confidential company information must use the company-approved VPN when working remotely.

Managers may require employees to attend the office for important meetings, training sessions, or business requirements.
